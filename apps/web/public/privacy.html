Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

This app (PTV LMS) helps freelance teachers run their classes: courses, lessons, attendance, homework, grades, and fee receipts. Each teacher manages their own students. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

We do not use passwords. You sign in with Google, or with a one-time link sent to your email.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address (needed to sign in and to know who you are).
  • If you are a teacher: your students' names, emails, and phone numbers (only the ones you add); your own bank details, only if you choose to add them, to show a payment QR code on receipts.
  • If you are a student: your attendance, homework answers, scores, and fee receipts, as your teacher enters them.
  • Basic technical information: your sign-in session, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your IP address, used only to slow down abuse such as repeated wrong sign-in attempts. We never store your real IP address, and there is no password to store.

Who sees it

Your teacher sees the information for their own students only. Teachers cannot see other teachers' students. The people who run this app can see the data stored in the database, only to keep the app running and fix problems. We do not read it for any other reason, sell it, or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Google, only to confirm who you are when you sign in with Google. We only ask for your name, email, and profile picture — nothing else.
  • Our email provider, only to send you sign-in links, invites, and fee receipts.
  • Telegram, only if a teacher chooses to link their Telegram account, so notifications can also be sent there.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions and one-time links are removed automatically after they expire or go unused for a while.
  • Attendance, homework, scores, and receipts are kept as your teacher's records, for as long as the teacher uses this app.
  • If a teacher deletes a piece of homework, the record is hidden but the score history is kept, because it must never change after the fact.

Your rights

If you are a teacher, you are in charge of your own students' data: you can edit, invite, or remove it, except finished records that must stay as they are (such as a sent receipt or a graded score history). If you want your personal data removed, ask your teacher (if you are a student), or contact us directly (if you are a teacher). We will remove what we can, keeping only what basic accounting or the law requires.

No advertising

We do not run ads, and we do not track you across other websites.
