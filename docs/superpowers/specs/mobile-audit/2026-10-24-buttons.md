Scope: every `vi-m-*.png` in `the screenshot sweep (scripts/i18n/screens.mjs) output` (34 files), checked against the templates in `apps/web/src`. Paths below are relative to `apps/web/src/`. Line numbers are template lines as of `feat/mobile-buttons` @ 66cf573.

- Some states or tabs have no screenshot: the Students, Lessons, Homework and Links tabs of the course detail, the draft invoice, the hand-in bar on My Work, dialogs other than feedback, and the import preview. Findings for these come from the source only and are marked **(source)**.

## 1. Proposed rules (mobile = below `sm`, 640px)

**Root cause found during the audit.** `AppCard`'s body is `flex flex-col`, so an `AppButton` placed directly in a card stretches to full width. That is why the Invite form and the Invoice actions look right. Almost every left-stranded button sits inside an extra `<div>` (for example `<div><AppButton/></div>`), which stops it from stretching. Placement therefore depends on wrapper markup, not on a rule. The fixes below make the design system own the rule.

| Context | Mobile (<640px) | Desktop (≥640px) |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 Page header actions** (`AppPage #actions`) | Title block on top. Actions go on their own full-width row under it. Regular-size buttons share the row equally (`flex-1`): one button is full width, two buttons are 50/50, and the main button is last, so it sits on the right (keeps the architecture rule). Show **at most 2** buttons. A third or rarer action (delete, archive) goes into a "⋯" overflow menu. Status badges and due text are not actions: they move to a new `#meta` slot under the title. | Unchanged: actions on the right of the title, main button last. |
| **R2 Form submit rows** | Primary is full width **on top**, and the secondary (Hủy / Quay lại) is full width **under it** (`flex-col-reverse`, DOM order secondary then primary). Why: the primary is the first thing read and sits nearest the thumb after the last field. Two equal full-width rows never go ragged with long Vietnamese labels ("Kiểm tra danh sách", "Gửi bài lại cho học viên"). Cancel is kept away from the primary, so it is harder to tap by mistake. This is the same order as dialogs (R4). A single submit button is simply full width. | Right-aligned row, secondary then primary (primary at the far right), the same as dialogs. A `align="start"` escape hatch remains for the rare left-aligned case. |
| **R3 Card-level actions** (`AppCard #actions`) | Stay compact, **always right-aligned** in the card header. If the title is long they wrap to a second line that is still right-aligned (`ms-auto justify-end`), never under the title on the left. A card's own submit button follows R2. | Unchanged. |
| **R4 Dialog and confirm footers** (`AppModal`, `AppConfirm`) | Bottom sheet (`modal-bottom`). Buttons stacked full width, primary or danger on top and cancel last. This also covers the 3-button "cancel lesson" dialogs. | Centred modal (`sm:modal-middle`), right-aligned row, primary last. |
| **R5 List-row actions** | Compact ghost buttons (or icon buttons with `aria-label`) grouped **at the right edge** of the row. If they wrap under the row content, the group is `basis-full justify-end`. Never indented on the left. | Inline at the right of the row. |
| **R6 Sticky action bars** (attendance save, hand in) | Status text on one small line, main button full width under it. Respects the safe area (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`). | Text left, button right (as today). |
| **R7 Small inline controls stay compact** | Pager prev/next, "load more", "Chọn tất cả / Bỏ chọn", icon buttons, row "Sửa / Hủy", week and month arrows. They keep their compact look and are **not** stretched. They are placed at the right, or in pairs spread to both edges (pager, period navigators). "Add another item" adders inside a form ("＋ Thêm câu trả lời", "＋ Thêm liên kết") are the exception: on mobile they become full-width outline rows, so they are not stranded on the left. | Compact and inline. |
| **R8 Touch targets** | Every tappable control is at least 44px tall. Compact buttons keep their 32px look on desktop but get `min-h-11` below `sm`. `AppSegmented` options grow to 44px and can fill the width. | Compact `btn-sm` is allowed. |
| **R9 Empty-state call to action** | Full width, inside the empty block (max-w-xs). | Centred, natural width. |

Visual identity (colours, fonts, radii, variants) does not change. Only layout and size classes change.
